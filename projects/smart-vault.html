Smart Vault — Document Vault

A fast, 100% offline document manager. Scan, organize, search, and protect — everything stays on-device.

Get it on Google Play

Overview

Smart Vault is a fast, private, 100% offline document manager. Scan, organize, search, and protect your important documents — everything stays on your device. No account, no cloud, no analytics.

Your Aadhaar, PAN, passport, licence, bills, and receipts never leave your phone.

Text inside your documents is read using on-device OCR via Google ML Kit, so images never leave the device while everything stays instantly searchable.

Features

  • ✓Document scanner with automatic edge detection and cropping
  • ✓On-device OCR (Google ML Kit) for instant full-text search
  • ✓Smart auto-categorization: Aadhaar, PAN, Passport, Licence, Receipts, Insurance, and more
  • ✓Full-text search across titles, notes, and scanned text
  • ✓App lock with PIN and optional fingerprint/biometric unlock
  • ✓Favorites, archiving, and personal notes on any document
  • ✓Export to PDF and share directly from the app
  • ✓Clean Material Design interface with light and dark modes
