Markly — Highlight & Save Any Page

Highlight text on any page. Your marks come back when you return, all searchable in one panel.

Get on Chrome Web Store

Overview

Markly turns any web page into something you can mark up — and actually find again. Select a sentence, pick a colour, and it's saved. Come back tomorrow, next week or next month and your highlights are exactly where you left them.

No account, no sync service, no sign-up — everything lives in your own browser.

Markly doesn't remember where on the page you highlighted — it remembers the words. So when a site restyles, re-renders, or loads content in a different order, your highlights still land on the right sentence.

Features

  • ✓Five highlight colours, plus fill, underline, or strikethrough styles
  • ✓Attach notes to any highlight — searchable alongside the quote
  • ✓One side panel for a page's highlights or everything you've ever marked, grouped by page
  • ✓Highlight via toolbar, right-click menu, or Alt+Shift+H
  • ✓Highlights survive page restyles, re-renders, and reordered content
  • ✓Export everything as Markdown or as a restorable JSON backup
  • ✓Dark-site aware, works with infinite scroll and single-page apps
