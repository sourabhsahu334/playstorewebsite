Yonder — Currency, Unit, Time Zone & Translation

Highlight any price, measurement, time, or foreign text to convert or translate it instantly.

Get on Chrome Web Store

Overview

Ever hit a price in the wrong currency, a distance in the wrong units, or a meeting time in the wrong zone — and had to stop and open a converter? Yonder does it in place.

Highlight any price, measurement, or time on any web page and Yonder pops a tiny card with the converted value — in your currency, your unit system, and your time zone. Click any result to copy it.

The content of the pages you visit is never read or transmitted — Yonder only looks at the text you select, and only to convert it.

Features

  • ✓150+ currencies with live daily exchange rates, plus an offline bundled rate snapshot
  • ✓Time zone conversion with DST awareness and custom cities
  • ✓Length, weight, temperature, speed, and volume conversion (metric ⇄ imperial)
  • ✓Smart parsing of symbols, ISO codes, decimal conventions, and magnitude suffixes ($1.5k, 2M)
  • ✓Fully translated interface in 13 languages, including RTL support for Arabic
  • ✓Vanilla JavaScript with no frameworks or build step
