Current Affairs 2026 — GK World

An educational companion for India GK and current affairs, built for school students.

Get it on Google Play

Overview

GK World is the ultimate educational app designed to empower school students with a wealth of knowledge about India GK and beyond. In a world where information is key, GK World serves as your invaluable guide, unlocking doors to academic excellence and a deeper understanding of India and the world.

The app is crafted to complement your school curriculum — a personal study companion aligned with your academic needs — while also covering India's history, culture, and geography, and offering a broader global perspective on international affairs.

Slogan: "Unlocking Wisdom, One Question at a Time."

Features

  • ✓Curriculum-aligned academic content for school students
  • ✓Deep dives into India's history, culture, geography, and heritage
  • ✓Global perspective: international geography, influential figures, and world issues
  • ✓Designed to build daily knowledge habits and exam readiness
